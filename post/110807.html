The Shu Road is rugged, the forests are lush, the birds chirp, and the streams burble. Where the scenery is enchanting, the sword stays sheathed.
Ten years ago, I studied physics and information technology at one of the most prestigious universities in my province. I often went to the department’s computer room, and over time I became familiar with the attractive female teacher who managed it. Though she was called a teacher, she looked only about twenty-five or twenty-six. She was slightly plump and, supposedly, already married. Her husband seemed to be a businessman who was often away on business trips.
Back then, the department’s computer room used cards, unlike today’s card-swiping and QR-code scanning systems. The teacher had several cards for public use. Once, while I was online in the computer room, the attractive administrator suddenly called out, “So-and-so, bring me my card.” So-and-so replied, “Wait, I haven’t finished inserting it yet.”
So the attractive administrator came over herself to retrieve the card, and several male students who had been using the computers stood up and gathered around her.
Student A said, “Teacher, I need to insert your card too.”
Student B said, “Teacher, I want to insert you—every day.”
Student C seemed to take the opportunity to touch the attractive teacher’s buttocks.
……
"The attractive teacher didn’t bat an eyelid: “I’m not letting you insert them anymore. You’ve all ruined the cards by inserting them.”"
I felt a wave of disappointment. The teacher had also looked out for me, but I was rather awkward and didn’t know how to strike up a conversation.
Once, I saw a male student enter the attractive teacher’s office, after which they closed the door from inside. For nearly the following hour, students who came to use the internet were turned away.
